# Primordial Sparks: Artistic Data Visualization & ALife Simulation

## Core Philosophy
A computational exploration of cosmogony and emergent life starting from absolute stillness (Wuji). A single primordial spark awakens in the void, expends energy through radiant non-uniform shockwaves, exhausts its reserves, and undergoes cellular fission. As entities multiply, they interact via kinship filaments, forage for ambient manna, and navigate carrying capacity.

## Current State: Version 2.0 (with Manna Economy)
- **Engine:** D3.js (v7) on an infinite SVG viewport with `d3.zoom()` (pan & scroll-wheel zoom 0.2x to 10x).
- **Physiology:** Humanized life-stage progression (infancy, prime radiance, conservation), stamina recovery homeostasis, and dynamic drive accumulation.
- **Reproduction:** Mitotic fission into twin offspring upon complete energy expenditure or abundance threshold.
- **Resource Economy:** Gaussian resource basins (rich manna veins) + diffuse ambient dust, grazed via proximity pull and metabolic drain.
- **Relational Aesthetics:** Kinship filaments with distance tension, historical lineage memory trails, multi-stage shockwaves, and standalone SVG export.

## Roadmap & Next Objectives
1. **Genome Implementation:**
   - `temperament` (-1.0 Deep Introvert to +1.0 Radical Extravert): Solitude stamina recharge vs. cluster stamina recharge.
   - `magnetism` (0.2 to 2.0): Charisma, gravitational pull, visual aura weight, and tribal leadership.
   - `longevity` & biological wear: Aging curves and twilight elder transitions.
2. **Mortality & Hazards:** Congenital viability (stillbirth/frailty) and collision shock leading to dormant cold ash husks.
3. **Artifact Production:** Sparks expending surplus energy to anchor static geometric beacons, glyphs, or energy caches into the void.
4. **Tribal Dynamics:** Boids-style flocking (alignment, cohesion, inter-clan repulsion).
